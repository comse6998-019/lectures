Literature proposed for the planning section

  • Planning and replanning: Song et al., LLM-Planner, ICCV 2023. High-level action sequences are revised using observed objects and completed subgoals. Its embodied simulation supplies a concrete planning mechanism; the context-retention implications are a course interpretation.
  • Extremely long simulated horizons: Vending-Bench and Vending-Bench 2, 2025. The successor follows a business over a simulated year, with delayed effects and ongoing commitments. Its horizon is simulated calendar time; its score concerns continued business operation and final money balance.
  • Continuity across context windows: Justin Young / Anthropic, Effective harnesses for long-running agents, November 2025. This first-party web-app engineering report connects incremental plans, progress records, and verification. Its experiments motivate examining the information a compacted context must carry into a later session.

Scope for discussion: use long and extremely long horizons as descriptive categories, with each source's actual horizon stated. A plan's depth, an agent's wall-clock runtime, and a simulated year are separate measurements. This week examines planning and information requirements; next week develops state ownership, durable checkpoints, resume, and replay.

2. Persist the complete plan and review it in Plannotator.
3. After plan approval, discuss and persist the detailed spec.
4. Review the detailed spec separately in Plannotator.
5. After both approvals, produce the slides and review the resulting section.

Use separate files for each section: `section-01-plan.md`, then `section-01-spec.md`, and so on. Record plan approval and spec approval separately. Reopen the relevant document when the instructor changes a decision.

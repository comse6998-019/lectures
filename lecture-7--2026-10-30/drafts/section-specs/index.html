Current status: the overview's chat green light is recorded. Section 1's plan is being developed through instructor questions. No section has both plan and spec approval.

The instructor determines which sections belong, their content, and their flow. We review the plan first. After plan approval, we walk through the details and review the detailed spec. Both decisions are recorded before slide work begins.

What each spec records

  1. The section's objective, prerequisites, scope, and connection to adjacent sections.
  2. The instructor's decisions and the open questions still to settle.
  3. The agreed teaching sequence, intended slide content, and claims with their sources and qualifications.
  4. Exact worked examples, demonstrations, predictions, and debriefs, including preparation and fallback choices.
  5. Figure, build, and layout instructions; speaker explanations; timings and cuts.
  6. The separate plan and spec review results and any conditions attached to either approval.

Review sequence

Overall structure approval → section-plan walkthrough → persisted plan → plan approval → detailed-spec walkthrough → persisted spec → spec approval → slide production → Plannotator review of the resulting section.

Keep a separate pair of files for each section: section-01-plan.html and section-01-spec.html, and so on. The status records plan discussion/approval, spec discussion/approval, and implementation separately. A changed decision reopens the relevant plan or spec.
